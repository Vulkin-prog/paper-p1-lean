# A fixed positive prime-mass bound gives a quantitative actual sieve error

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

Three theorems prove the actual normalized avoidance bound <=2/M+(card(P)^2/N)/M^2 when reciprocal mass>=M>0. For sequences with actual card(P)^2/N tending to zero, the literal avoidance density is eventually <=2/M+epsilon. Adding ANY proved negligible square-exception family preserves that bound with its error accounted for. No divergence of M or unproved density conclusion is assumed.

The triple-large prime frequency contribution, the central-frequency mean comparison and complete logarithmic short-average concentration remain unproved.
