# Actual arbitrary-anchor unsigned field and uniform retained mass

Sources: companion T19.16 and T19.17, limits_appendix.tex:1214 and1313, including the clean-pair constructions in their proofs. All original source hashes are preserved in the numbered ledger.

Companion T19.17 now has the actual non-signed field, for EVERY
admissible arithmetic anchor rule on its original dyadic population,
with complete Palm identities, exact selected clean-pair offsite costs,
and a proved uniform retained-mass bound. The final obstruction remains
in progress; no ordinary smooth short-interval theorem is assumed proved.

AnchoredPrivateField takes the anchor as a fixed function of the integer
boundary before the random prime sample. At each site the already proved
AnchoredWindowForcing data require only the nonanchor primes to be
private. The anchor value may be defective or deterministic. The actual
Boolean event is the two-orientation orbit of the actual raw word.
Its rate is exactly 2^(-K) for a word of length K+1. The actual map
hits the event, has its complete conditional sample law, and satisfies
the full weighted Palm identity for every real prime-cylinder observable.

The total marginal is exactly |G|*2^(-K). The actual full source-deleted
coordinate cost decomposes into |G|*2^(-2K) plus the offsite cost. The
latter is the quantity used in T19.17, so the diagonal is not inserted
into its lower bound.

For every clean directed pair with a surjective JOINT raw matrix, the
physical anchored prime change has expected target-indicator change
exactly 2^(-K), and exact source-weighted contribution 2^(-2K). The
actual selected-pair offsite minorant retains every such contribution.
The clean divisibility predicates, actual joint matrix condition and
selected nonanchor prime are explicit finite data; the word law,
Palm identity, fair correction bit and cost value are proved. This
criterion applies to the original arithmetic forcing, not a substitute
flip model. No fairness of the preserved anchor bit is required.

AnchoredDyadicField defines the literal original population: dyadic
left boundaries whose maximal support contains at most one pivot at
most Y. For every shorter actual word, an admissible arithmetic anchor
exists. Every rule that anchors the small pivot when present constructs
the actual field data in every containing prime cylinder. The orbit
indicators for the two templates are identified with the actual run
start and unsigned exact-excess-zero event. Their rates are respectively
p=2^(-L) and p/2=2^(-(L+1)). All rules are quantified before sampling.

Every maximal word whose pivots all exceed Z>=Y belongs to this original
retained population. Its exact finite cover gives

  N <= |G| + #badBoundaries(N,B,Z).

AnchoredRetainedMass uses the proved Rankin count to select a threshold
before all B<=D*log N, Y<=Z<=log(N)^5, cylinder cutoffs, actual word
lengths, anchor rules and templates. For each actual field of rate q,

  (N-N^(9/10+epsilon))*q <= total actual mean <= N*q.

This restores the retained population with a quantitative, proved loss,
uniformly over arithmetic anchor choices. It does not supply two ordinary
N^a-smooth integers in almost every short interval, select the required
small forced source pivot, aggregate its clean surviving arithmetic
pairs into the offsite minorant, or prove the final N^(-a) obstruction.
The randomized-orientation extension also remains a separate task.
