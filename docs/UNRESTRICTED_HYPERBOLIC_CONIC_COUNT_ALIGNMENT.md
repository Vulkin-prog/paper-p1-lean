# Coefficient-height-free uniform counts for geometric hyperbolic conics

Source: main13.4 conic preparation. This closes the hyperbolic branch by the internally proved associated bounded-height presentation and geometric signed quadratic count.

UnrestrictedHyperbolicConicCount.natural_count proves the unconditional uniform N^epsilon bound for the actual signed integer grid of every geometrically irreducible integer plane equation of exact total degree two and positive discriminant, with no restriction on any original coefficient. The threshold is chosen before the equation and the actual grid radius N. All original coordinate signs and axis points remain included.

Populations of at most four points are absorbed uniformly. Every larger population internally constructs a bounded-height associated equation of the same exact degree and geometric irreducibility. The actual rational association preserves the positive discriminant and gives equality of every original and replacement grid. The proved interpolation coefficient bound is polynomial in N with constants fixed solely by degree two, so the already proved controlled-height conic theorem applies. Neither the normalized equation, association, sign preservation nor a counting input is supplied externally.

finite_population_count proves the same bound for every actual finite population of original zeros in the symmetric N-box. polynomial_height_count proves the full coefficient-unrestricted version in every fixed polynomial coordinate-height box B<=N^K. Its threshold precedes every original equation and B. The small-box case is controlled by the actual grid at the fixed threshold; the large-box case uses the exact real-power budget epsilon/(K+1).
