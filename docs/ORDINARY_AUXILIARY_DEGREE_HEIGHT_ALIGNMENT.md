# Actual homogeneous dilation, chart bijections and ordinary curve height at every positive auxiliary degree

Source: companionT1.7, arithmetic_appendix.tex:480, ordinary surface-cover conversion and its independent auxiliary-degree quantifier. Original manuscript hashes remain unchanged.

HomogeneousDilation constructs an actual coefficient-algebra endomorphism in every genuinely graded commutative coefficient algebra. On an actual homogeneous element of degree n, it acts by multiplication by b^(k*n). Its linear homogeneous components extend through the actual direct-sum algebra decomposition; the unit and multiplicative laws are proved explicitly. No polynomial presentation, domain, normality or standard-generation assumption is required.

GradedVeroneseDilation takes k=D-1 for D>0 and a genuine degree-one coordinate b. Its literal image belongs to the actual Dth Veronese subalgebra, with a source-degree-n element mapping into genuine Veronese degree n and underlying original degree n*D. It constructs the actual graded algebra map, proves its exact homogeneous-element formula and proves that b maps to the actual coordinate b^D.

GradedVeroneseDilationChart proves that the actual induced normalized homogeneous-chart map is bijective, including in rings with zero divisors. Injectivity clears a literal power of the chart coordinate after taking an actual homogeneous fraction representative. Surjectivity constructs a preimage of a target degree-n fraction using its numerator as a source element of degree n*D; the exact exponent identity proves equality of the actual localized fractions. Existing actual chart-localization theorems then give bijectivity of the full localized ring map as well. No chart equivalence is an input.

GradedVeroneseChartPrimeHeight proves that a homogeneous prime avoiding b is recovered exactly by pulling its Veronese contraction back along the dilation. Membership is compared on actual homogeneous elements; the normalizer power cancels by primality. The actual localized-ring bijection therefore proves that this prime and its literal contraction to every positive Dth Veronese have exactly equal heights. This theorem applies to arbitrary genuinely graded commutative coefficient algebras and does not require the ambient algebra to be a domain or normal.

OrdinaryCurveVeroneseHeight constructs the actual prime of the ordinary radical-surface quotient defined by a given ordinary curve ideal, proves its homogeneity and chart avoidance, identifies each actual auxiliary-degree surface ideal with its literal Veronese contraction, and applies the general height theorem. Thus the actual ordinary surface-curve height is independent of every positive auxiliary Veronese degree. OrdinarySurfaceCurveRegrading constructs the corresponding actual curve at any such degree with exactly the same polynomial ideal, natural Hilbert degree, Hilbert polynomial and radical equations. Its source_curve_at_degree theorem upgrades the actual source-to-ordinary curve construction: a source curve at its chosen embedding E produces an actual ordinary image curve at arbitrary positive auxiliary D, with the fixed literal image ideal and genuine bounds d <= E_source <= E*d. There is no restriction relating auxiliary D to the sizes of the radical equations.
