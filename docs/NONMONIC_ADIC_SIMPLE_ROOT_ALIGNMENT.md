# Simple-root lifting without a monicity or leading-coefficient assumption

Source: companion T1.4 non-geometric branch proof development; Mathlib/RingTheory/Henselian.lean at commit9d581dfff2e41de989072ea63abf3daf69311520, IsAdicComplete.henselianRing (Johan Commelin, Apache2.0). Original manuscript hashes remain preserved.

The formal-branch route to the remaining non-geometric algebraic implication no longer needs a nonvanishing leading coefficient at the selected rational point. A new unconditional adic lifting theorem applies to every polynomial over an I-adically complete commutative ring: if its value at a0 lies in I and its derivative at a0 is a unit modulo I, then an actual root congruent to a0 exists. No monicity or leading-coefficient unit is assumed.

The Newton/completeness proof is adapted from Mathlib/RingTheory/Henselian.lean, IsAdicComplete.henselianRing, at the pinned commit9d581dfff2e41de989072ea63abf3daf69311520. The upstream monic proof argument is unused in that proof; the new theorem exposes exactly the hypotheses actually used. Its Newton iteration, derivative units, successive ideal-power errors, Cauchy property and complete/Hausdorff limiting argument are checked again by Lean under the pinned compiler. The module retains Johan Commelin attribution, the exact upstream file and commit, and Apache2.0 terms; a byte-identical copy of the upstream licence is in docs/third_party/mathlib-APACHE-2.0.txt. This is not a Paper C port.

Specializing the theorem to PowerSeries K for any field K gives a root with a prescribed simple constant-term root for every power-series-coefficient polynomial. The proof needs only the actual constant-term root and derivative nonvanishing. The earlier PowerSeriesHenselLift.exists_root theorem is preserved; this new version removes its extra leading-coefficient unit condition. Thus a future smooth-point branch construction can use the selected point's local derivative condition directly, even when the polynomial loses leading degree there.
