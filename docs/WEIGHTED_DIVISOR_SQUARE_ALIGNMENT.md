# Exact weighted divisor square mean retains every least common multiple cross term

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

For any finite family of positive integer moduli d and nonnegative real weights w, the literal divisor sum D(n)=sum(d|n,w(d)) has exact square expansion over pairs with lcm(d,e)|n. Its ACTUAL sum on 1<=n<=X is exactly sum(d,e,floor(X/lcm(d,e))*w(d)*w(e)), and hence <=X*K with K=sum(d,e,w(d)*w(e)/lcm(d,e)). Nonnegativity of D and K is proved. Five theorems and two definitions preserve every repeated or overlapping divisor term; no independence approximation or unidentified external moment input is used.

The complementary bad-prime frequency contribution, the central-frequency mean subtraction and complete logarithmic short-average concentration remain unproved.
