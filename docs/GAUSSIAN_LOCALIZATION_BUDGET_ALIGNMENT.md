# Actual finite logarithmic Gaussian averages have a proved one-candidate localization budget

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

Five theorems prove the actual Gaussian exponential comparison and weighted finite tail sum using the checked unweighted logarithmic Gaussian mass bound. A whole-sum bound and an actual at-most-one-candidate bound are derived. Every finite population split into at most one inside candidate and a distant outside population has an explicit total upper bound, including its exact Gaussian tail factor, prefactor and rounding-free finite sums. No smooth-number or window-census input is assumed.

The actual off-central estimate, the ordinary smooth long-interval mean comparison and the uniform Gaussian mean-square comparison are proved. Localization of the Gaussian averages to the literal logarithmic windows remains unproved, so T19.17 stays conditional. The stronger uniform sharp short-average theorem for all real completely multiplicative functions is not established. T19.17 conditional; original T1.7 unconditional.
