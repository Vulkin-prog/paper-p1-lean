# Actual logarithmic window cells have exact gap disjointness and volume controls

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

Six theorems and one definition prove upper and lower logarithmic ratio bounds with all endpoint conditions, the explicit distance gap outside an integer window for every center in its middle unit cell, measurability of the negative-logarithmic cells, their exact lower real measure 1/M under the actual endpoint bound, and pairwise disjointness for distinct integer indices. All integers, half-open endpoints and real centers are literal.

The actual off-central estimate, the ordinary smooth long-interval mean comparison and the uniform Gaussian mean-square comparison are proved. Localization of the Gaussian averages to the literal logarithmic windows remains unproved, so T19.17 stays conditional. The stronger uniform sharp short-average theorem for all real completely multiplicative functions is not established. T19.17 conditional; original T1.7 unconditional. Coverage: 164 proved, 1 conditional, 2 in_progress /167; all 73 main proved. Sources, false-scope corrections, compiler and axioms unchanged. About 52.08 GiB free on Ubuntu.
