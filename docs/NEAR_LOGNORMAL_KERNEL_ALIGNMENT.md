# The actual smoothed integer Mellin kernel has an explicit three-quarter-power near-frequency error

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

Four theorems and one definition prove C_near>0 and the actual full infinite-series Mellin difference bound <=C_near*A^(3/4) for A>=2 and |t|<=A^(1/4). The exact complex Gaussian main term, including its phase, is retained, and the literal series norm bound follows. This is derived from the complete proved quadrature-and-tail estimate; no near-frequency kernel bound is assumed.

The triple-large prime frequency contribution, the central-frequency mean comparison and complete logarithmic short-average concentration remain unproved.
