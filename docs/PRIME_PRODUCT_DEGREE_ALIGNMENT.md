# Exact prime product layers are characterized by support and total valuation degree

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

For finite prime P the ACTUAL k-product image layer consists exactly of positive integers supported on P whose total P-adic valuation is k. Total valuation is additive on every finite nonzero product; every member of the product layer has prime support P and degree k. Conversely strong induction on the positive integer constructs a literal prime tuple from its supported factorization, including repeated prime factors and the degree-zero integer 1. Five theorems and one support definition prove this exact characterization.

The complementary bad-prime frequency contribution, the central-frequency mean subtraction and complete logarithmic short-average concentration remain unproved.
