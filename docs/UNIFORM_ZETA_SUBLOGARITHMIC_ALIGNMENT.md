# The actual Riemann zeta function is uniformly sublogarithmic for every positive damping up to one

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

Three theorems prove: for EVERY epsilon>0, ONE T0>=8 precedes ALL |t|>=T0 and ALL 0<sigma<=1, with actual norm(zeta(1+sigma+it))<=epsilon*log |t|. The normalized form is proved as well. For every fixed B>0, the same gives norm<=epsilon*log N uniformly on T0<=|t|<=N^B, N>=1 and ALL 0<sigma<=1. The free lower-cutoff exponent is chosen AFTER epsilon and the normalized explicit envelope is then taken to its actual limit. No zeta estimate, prime estimate or MR concentration is assumed as input.

The triple-large prime frequency contribution, the central-frequency mean comparison and complete logarithmic short-average concentration remain unproved.
