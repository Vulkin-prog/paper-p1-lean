# A precision-selected fixed-power population has the required actual Ramare support and small prescribed exceptional density

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

Five theorems and one definition prove actual good-set inclusion, ordinary first-band and final-band divisibility counts and square exclusion, an exact exceptional-union density bound, and disjointness of the actual final prime band from the first prime band for every fixed positive lower power. For EVERY epsilon>0, a fixed a in (0,1/32) is chosen first and the actual combined exceptional density is eventually <=epsilon. The quantifier order is retained; no density-zero assertion for a fixed power band is made.

The triple-large prime frequency contribution, the central-frequency mean comparison and complete logarithmic short-average concentration remain unproved.
