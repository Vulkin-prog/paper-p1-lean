# Actual least common multiples of two prime product layers remain in degrees k through 2k

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

The total valuation of lcm(a,b) lies between max(degree(a),degree(b)) and degree(a)+degree(b), by the exact coordinatewise maximum of their factorizations. Prime support is preserved. Consequently the ACTUAL least-common-multiple image of two k-product layers lies in the union of the literal layers j=k,...,2*k. Four theorems prove the valuation bounds, support, membership and finite image inclusion, retaining all shared and repeated factors.

The complementary bad-prime frequency contribution, the central-frequency mean subtraction and complete logarithmic short-average concentration remain unproved.
