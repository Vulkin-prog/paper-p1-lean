# The actual Gaussian-difference frequency energy is uniformly small on neighboring dyadic blocks

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

Two theorems prove that for every fixed D>0 and epsilon>0, one integer cutoff precedes all N, every M in [N,2N], every real completely multiplicative prime-unit function and the actual widths chosen at N. The whole Gaussian-difference frequency energy of the literal polynomial on [M,2M) is at most epsilon. A same-base corollary is included. The actual off-central theorem, central limit and Gaussian tail are assembled with all scale choices before varying functions. No frequency or mean-square concentration estimate is assumed.

The actual off-central Dirichlet estimate and the ordinary smooth long-interval mean comparison are proved. The analytic comparison with logarithmic short averages remains unproved, so T19.17 remains conditional. The stronger uniform short-average theorem for all real completely multiplicative functions is not established. T19.17 conditional; original T1.7 unconditional.
