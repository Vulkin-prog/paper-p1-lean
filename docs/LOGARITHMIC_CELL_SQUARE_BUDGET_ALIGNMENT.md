# Actual disjoint logarithmic cells convert square energy into a finite exceptional-count budget

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

Two theorems prove a literal cell square-integral lower bound from a pointwise absolute gap, and the exact finite population inequality card(B)*kappa^2/M <= integral G(u)^2. The proof uses the actual disjoint negative-logarithmic cells, their proved measure lower bound, finite-union integral additivity and whole-line nonnegativity. The function square is explicitly integrable and every pointwise gap is an explicit hypothesis; no smooth-number or concentration estimate is postulated.

The actual off-central estimate, the ordinary smooth long-interval mean comparison and the uniform Gaussian mean-square comparison are proved. Localization of the Gaussian averages to the literal logarithmic windows remains unproved, so T19.17 stays conditional. The stronger uniform sharp short-average theorem for all real completely multiplicative functions is not established. T19.17 conditional; original T1.7 unconditional.
