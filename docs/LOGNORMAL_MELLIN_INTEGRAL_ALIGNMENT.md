# The actual lognormal Mellin integral has an exact Gaussian formula

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

Six theorems and one definition prove the actual phase identities, Gaussian kernel integral and positive-domain logarithmic substitution. For every A>0 and every real t, the literal integral of w_A(x)*exp(-it log x) over x>0 equals A*phase(-log A,t)*sqrt(pi)*exp(-t^2/4), with its exact norm A*sqrt(pi)*exp(-t^2/4). All constants and phases are retained, including t=0. No smooth Mellin transform formula is assumed.

The triple-large prime frequency contribution, the central-frequency mean comparison and complete logarithmic short-average concentration remain unproved.
