PolynomialAuxiliaryExceptions proves a finite-family Bezout theorem over every infinite field. Its actual exceptional locus consists of P=0 together with a zero of some auxiliary polynomial not divisible by P. Every finite population has size at most deg(P) times the sum of the actual auxiliary degrees, and a genuine finite set with exactly that membership is constructed. Divisible auxiliaries are omitted from this locus and proved zero on the entire actual curve. On any subset of the curve avoiding the locus, each auxiliary restriction is therefore identically zero or nowhere zero. No independence or nonzero-auxiliary premise is imposed on the family.

GraphControlPolynomials constructs the concrete two-orientation graph-control family. Its ten static polynomials are P_x, P_y, P_x+P_y, P_x-P_y, x-1, x+1, y-1, y+1, x and y. The last two retain the zeroth-order coordinate restrictions explicitly. The other 2r polynomials are the exact positive-order numerators for y as a function of x and x as a function of y, through order r, using the corrected internally proved recurrence and exact coordinate transposition. Every actual degree is at most 1+2 deg(P)(r+1), the degree sum has an explicit (10+2r) allowance, and the total Bezout budget is at most 30 deg(P)^2 (r+1)^2 for positive-degree P.

GraphControlExceptions constructs the genuine finite set for this full family at every irreducible real plane equation of positive degree. It retains exact membership and the uniform bound E.card<=30 deg(P)^2 (r+1)^2. All singular curve points are already contained in it: the internally proved actual nonzero partial of strictly smaller degree cannot be divisible by P, and its evaluation vanishes at every singular point. Thus every actual curve point outside this set is nonsingular without an additional singular exceptional allowance. Every concrete control restriction has the exact zero-or-nowhere-zero dichotomy on every subset avoiding E.
