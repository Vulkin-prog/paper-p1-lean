Apply Lemma~\ref{p13:transfer:independent-tail} with \(v=u\) and \(a=1\),
and increase its fixed cutoff to \(s_0\ge s\) if necessary.
On \(G\) the exact marginals equal \(p\). Each set of \(r\) distinct
starts has exactly \(r!\) orderings, so the factor \(1/r!\) cancels
exactly. The contribution with \(r\ge s_0\) is therefore bounded by
the unordered tail of that lemma, \(O(N^{-1+\eta})\).
For the finitely many \(s\le r<s_0\), apply
Proposition~\ref{p1:arith:fixed-support} with all \(r\) projections nonzero.
It gives \(|\mathcal H_{r,N}|\ll N^{r/2+\eta}\), hence a weighted
contribution \(O_{r,u,C,\eta}(N^{-r/2+\eta})\).
Since \(r/2\ge1>2s/(2s+1)\), choosing \(\eta>0\) inside the requested
margin and summing these finitely many layers proves the result.
\end{proof}

Texte proposé pour toute la preuve, companion_sections/supplemental_arithmetic.tex:225–277 :

\begin{proof}
The matching calculation before the two channel specializations in
the proof of Lemma~\ref{p1:arith:palmrank} applies to any fixed coprime
\(b/2<a<b\). Write \(x=at-i_0\), \(y=bt-j_0\), and an aligned target as
\(z=(ct+h)/d\), with \((c,d)=1\) and polynomially bounded data.
Away from the stated \(B^{O(1)}\) endpoint sources, dyadic positions
and rational separation give \(b/2<c/d<2a\).
Its residual estimate and \eqref{p1:arith:matchdensity} bound the loss
by the displayed matching count plus \(o(L)\), also after exposing
the prime bits of \(a,b\). Only matches to true old values are counted.

Suppose \(c\nmid a\) and \(c\nmid b\), and put \(g=(c,a)\), \(h_1=(c,b)\).
These numbers are coprime and \(gh_1\mid c\).
If one equals one, the other is a proper divisor of \(c\), giving
density at most \(1/2\).
Otherwise \(g,h_1\geq2\) and
\[
 \frac{g+h_1-1}{c}
 \leq\frac1g+\frac1{h_1}-\frac1{gh_1}\leq\frac23.
\]
The last maximum occurs at the smallest coprime pair \(\{2,3\}\).
When \(c>b(a+b-1)\), the density is less than \(1/b\).
All other nonphysical fractions are included in
\eqref{p1:arith:gamma-fixed-channel}.

If \(c\mid a\), a proper divisor has \(c\leq a/2<b/2\), which is
impossible here; thus \(c=a,d=1\).
Similarly \(c\mid b\) leaves only \(c=b,d=1\).
These are the physical fractions reproducing one old family.
Compatibility gives distance at least \(L\) from that source, so the
two true windows share at most one value.
Matches to the other old family have steps \(a,b\) in intervals of
width \(B\), and number at most \(B/b+O(1)\).
This proves the rank bound in these cases as well.
\end{proof}
