# Actual long Gaussian averages of ordinary smooth integers are uniformly positive

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

One theorem proves that for every a>0, an actual kappa>0 and one integer cutoff precede all N and all real centers X with N<=X and X+1<=2N. The literal ordinary floor(N^a)-smooth Gaussian average at the actual long width is at least kappa. The proof combines the internal positive dyadic smooth density, proved uniform long-interval mean stability, exact ceil(X) window count and actual Gaussian subpopulation lower bound. No positivity or long local density estimate is supplied as a premise.

The actual off-central estimate, the ordinary smooth long-interval mean comparison and the uniform Gaussian mean-square comparison are proved. Localization of the Gaussian averages to the literal logarithmic windows remains unproved, so T19.17 stays conditional. The stronger uniform sharp short-average theorem for all real completely multiplicative functions is not established. T19.17 conditional; original T1.7 unconditional.
