Source: main13.4 plane preparation, internally proved geometric biform counts and bounded-height interpolation. This intermediate exponent4/D+epsilon is explicitly distinguished from the original BCN exponent1/D.

K is chosen before G and H; there is no coefficient-height assumption and no unproved counting input.

For the unrestricted theorem, populations of size at most D^2 are absorbed directly. Every larger population supplies an actually constructed integer equation Q with the same exact degree and geometric irreducibility, all original zeros, and polynomially bounded coefficient height. The finite interpolation and many-point descent proofs supply Q internally.
