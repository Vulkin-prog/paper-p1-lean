# A final prime band has divergent endpoints and a divergent harmonic gap

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

Define the ACTUAL final prime band by exp(log N/log(log N)) < p <= exp(log N/B). Its lower endpoint tends to infinity, its upper endpoint tends to infinity for each fixed positive B, its endpoints are eventually ordered above 2, and its harmonic logarithmic gap tends to infinity. The latter is eventually EXACTLY log(log(log N))-log B. Five theorems and three definitions retain the fixed-B quantifier order. This is a preparatory third band, not a bound for the remaining frequency contribution.

The joint large-prime frequency contribution, the central-frequency mean comparison and complete logarithmic short-average concentration remain unproved.
