# Actual finite real prime Euler products are uniformly small outside a fixed central frequency range

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

Two theorems prove: for every fixed B>0 and epsilon>0, ONE T0>=8 precedes ALL integers X>=2 with log X>=1, ALL finite prime sets P below X, ALL real prime coefficients bounded by one, and ALL T0<=|t|<=X^B; the literal product norm is <=epsilon*log X. Its fourth-power version is proved first. The proof combines the actual uniform damping cost, the corrected fourth-power complete zeta comparison, the proved positive real zeta bound and the proved sublogarithmic shifted zeta estimate. The near-zero range remains excluded and explicit. Multiplicativity is not required for the finite prime coefficients.

The triple-large prime frequency contribution, the central-frequency mean comparison and complete logarithmic short-average concentration remain unproved.
