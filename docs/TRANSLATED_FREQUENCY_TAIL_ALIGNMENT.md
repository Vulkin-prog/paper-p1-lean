# The literal translated inverse-square frequency tail has a vanishing bound

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

Three theorems prove the actual finite left and right frequency-weight tails, each <=2/R, and their complete combination <=4/R for ANY finite set of integer sample indices whose translated distance from the center is >=R>0. The actual reciprocal weights 1/(1+(j-K)^2), exact subtraction indices and zero-center exclusion are retained. There is no additive constant error in the tail bound.

The triple-large prime frequency contribution, the central-frequency mean comparison and complete logarithmic short-average concentration remain unproved.
