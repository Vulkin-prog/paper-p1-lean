# The actual lognormal sieve weight has a proved majorant and derivative

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

Six theorems and one definition construct the ACTUAL positive weight w_A(x)=exp(-log(x/A)-log(x/A)^2). They prove w<=exp(1/4), its exact identity (A/x)*exp(-log(x/A)^2) for positive A,x, the genuine majorant exp(2)*w>=1 on [A,2A], its polynomial tail w<=exp(1)*A^3/x^3, and its actual derivative w*(-(1+2 log(x/A))/x). Every positivity condition and the exact smoothing weight are explicit; no sparse prime Gram estimate is assumed.

The triple-large prime frequency contribution, the central-frequency mean comparison and complete logarithmic short-average concentration remain unproved.
