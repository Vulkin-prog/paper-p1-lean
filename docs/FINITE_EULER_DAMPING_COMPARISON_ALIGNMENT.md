# Changing positive radial Euler weights has a proved finite norm cost

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

Four theorems prove the real projection bound, exact coefficient real-part difference, and both logarithmic and norm comparisons for ANY actual finite Euler product when 0<=v_i<=w_i<=1/2, |f_i|<=1 and |z_i|=1. Replacing w by v costs at most sum(w-v)+2*sum(w^2) in the logarithm, or its actual exponential factor in norm. All local factors are proved nonzero and every quadratic error is retained.

The triple-large prime frequency contribution, the central-frequency mean comparison and complete logarithmic short-average concentration remain unproved.
