# The actual union of fixed-width large prime-block samples has a logarithmic-power cardinality bound

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

Two theorems bound the actual number of narrow blocks by (H+1)*log N and the actual union of large samples by (log N)^(200H+2). The conditions are explicit: all primes<=N, fixed H>=4, actual anchors>=H and anchor^H>=N, unit-separated samples at height T<=N, the literal threshold 1/(log N)^100, and a fixed numerical lower bound on log N. The finite union, each filtered frequency sample and every cardinality loss are retained.

The triple-large prime frequency contribution, the central-frequency mean comparison and complete logarithmic short-average concentration remain unproved.
