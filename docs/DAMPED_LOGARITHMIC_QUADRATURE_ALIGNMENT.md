# Actual damped sums admit an endpoint-independent sum-integral comparison

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

Eight theorems prove the exact derivative of the actual damped integrand and compare EVERY finite sum over Ico N M with its literal integral, for 0<N<=M and sigma>=0. The norm of the difference is <=2*(1+sigma+|t|)*N^(-sigma)/N, independently of M. For t nonzero, the sum norm is <=2*N^(-sigma)/|t| plus that error. The proof sums actual unit-cell errors and uses a proved reciprocal-square tail, rather than paying the interval length.

The triple-large prime frequency contribution, the central-frequency mean comparison and complete logarithmic short-average concentration remain unproved.
