Write $q_0(I)$ for the probability of the homogeneous events,
with zero right-hand side in place of the affine start prescription.
Exact factorization gives
\[
 q_0(I)=2^{\rho_{\rm rel}(I)}\prod_{x\in I}2^{-d_x}
       \le 2^r p^r2^{\rho_{\rm rel}(I)},\qquad |I|=r,
\]
because local rigidity gives $d_x\ge L-1$.
The nominal fixed-size estimates therefore imply the same powers
for the true homogeneous mass: $N^{-r/2+\varepsilon}$ in general
and $N^{1-r+\varepsilon}$ when $\rho_{\rm rel}\ge\eta L$.
Here $r$ and $\eta>0$ are fixed before $N$; the factor $2^r$
is absorbed using a smaller fixed error margin.
The original exact rank weights, actual joint probabilities,
and absolute centered coefficients are each bounded by $q_0(I)$.
Thus one homogeneous envelope controls all four sums, even when
an original affine system is incompatible or has a zero marginal.
