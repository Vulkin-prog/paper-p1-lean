# The actual rough population has a proved inverse-logarithmic density bound

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

Six theorems and two definitions combine the actual primorial Selberg sieve with exponent eta=min(a,1/8). For every a>0 and N>=2, the actual population up to 4N coprime to primorial(floor(N^a)) is at most (8/eta)*N/log(N)+sqrt(N). Consequently one integer cutoff gives the explicit bound (8/eta+1)*N/log(N). The proof retains exact floors, the literal sieve set and the polynomial sieve error. No rough-population asymptotic is assumed.

The actual off-central Dirichlet frequency estimate is proved. The central-frequency short-to-long comparison, the analytic short-average bridge and complete logarithmic short-average concentration remain unproved.
