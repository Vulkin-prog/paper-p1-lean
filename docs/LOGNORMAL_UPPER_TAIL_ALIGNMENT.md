# The actual infinite upper lognormal integer tail has a proved reciprocal-scale bound

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

Five theorems and one definition prove a finite reciprocal-cube tail bound <=8/M^2, the literal finite smoothed tail bound, absolute summability of the actual shifted positive-integer terms, and the infinite tail norm<=8*exp(1)*A^3/M^2. At the actual upper cutoff ceil(A^2), A>=2, this is <=8*exp(1)/A. All infinite limits and positive-integer indexing are justified.

The triple-large prime frequency contribution, the central-frequency mean comparison and complete logarithmic short-average concentration remain unproved.
