# Actual separated-frequency sampling and discrete polynomial large-value counts

Source: companion T19.17 and the separated-frequency large-value component of the independent multiplicative short-average route. Actual finite sample sets, derivative bandwidth and endpoint conventions are retained.

LocalSquareSampling proves, for every continuously differentiable complex function with an actual continuous derivative g, the literal point bound |f(a)|^2 <= 2*integral_[a,a+1]|f|^2 + integral_[a,a+1]|g|^2. It uses the actual derivative of the squared norm, the fundamental theorem of calculus and the inequality 2|inner(f,g)|<=|f|^2+|g|^2. The point evaluation estimate is proved, not supplied as a premise.

SeparatedIntervalBudget proves that the actual half-open unit intervals (tau_r,tau_r+1] attached to a finite set of frequency points at pairwise distance at least one are disjoint, including the endpoint-touching case. Their literal union lies in the asserted common interval. Finite union integration and positivity therefore bound the sum of local integrals by the common integral. It derives the finite sampled-square estimate with coefficients 2 and 1 for the function and derivative integrals. No count-to-measure assumption is used.

SeparatedPolynomialSampling computes the actual derivative of each complex exponential phase and of the finite exponential polynomial. For real frequencies with |w_n|<=W, its derivative coefficient-square sum is at most W^2 times the original square sum. Combining the internal continuous mean-value theorem and the proved local sampling bound gives, for every actual finite sample set R with tau_r in [-T,T] and pairwise distance at least one, sum_r |sum_n a_n exp(i*w_n*tau_r)|^2 <= exp(1)*(sqrt(pi)*(T+1)+2*pi/delta)*(2+W^2)*sum_n|a_n|^2, under the literal frequency gap delta*|j-i|<=|w_j-w_i| with delta>0 and T>0. For every V>0, the actual cardinality of any such sample set on which every value is >=V is at most the SAME expression divided by V^2. All derivatives, intervals, sums and cardinalities are actual; there is no unproved sampling or discrete-large-value premise. Three modules add nine theorems and no definitions.
