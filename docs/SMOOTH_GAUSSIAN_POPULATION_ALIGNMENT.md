# Literal ordinary smooth Gaussian averages equal their finite smooth population sums

Source: internal preparatory analytic route for companion T19.17. This is not a completed additional manuscript result.

Three theorems and one definition identify the literal [N,4N) ordinary smooth population, prove positivity of every member and prove that the actual Gaussian average of the exact smooth indicator equals its literal positive population sum with reciprocal weights. Every subpopulation lying within one Gaussian width gives an explicit cardinal lower bound for that actual average, with all centers, widths, finite supports and denominators retained. No smooth density or Gaussian lower bound is assumed.

The actual off-central estimate, the ordinary smooth long-interval mean comparison and the uniform Gaussian mean-square comparison are proved. Localization of the Gaussian averages to the literal logarithmic windows remains unproved, so T19.17 stays conditional. The stronger uniform sharp short-average theorem for all real completely multiplicative functions is not established. T19.17 conditional; original T1.7 unconditional.
