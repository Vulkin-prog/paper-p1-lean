# Actual total curve-degree budgets and a single auxiliary equation high-degree exception bound

Source: companionT1.7; the exact internally proved main13.4 quadratic-degree affine curve count and actual section-degree budgets. The single-auxiliary transfer is a new internal simplification, independent of any supplied covering theorem.

GradedSurfaceSectionCurveBudget retains the genuine total degree sum of the actual height-one curve components of a surface section: sum_Gamma degree(Gamma) <= e*d. The finite family is constructed from actual minimal primes, with the same original point coverage and individual Hilbert degrees. Passing from indexed components to their actual finite image uses nonnegativity of the curve degrees, so possible duplicate representatives do not inflate the sum. GradedSurfaceSectionCurves keeps its existing statement as a corollary of the stronger construction. P3HypersurfaceAuxiliaryDegreeBudget specializes that total budget to a literal degree-e rational auxiliary polynomial not divisible by the original P3 surface equation; P3HypersurfaceAuxiliaryCurves keeps its existing interface as a corollary.

PresentedCurveQuadraticCount transports the already proved main13.4 estimate to every actually presented projective curve, using its literal polynomial-coordinate ideal and genuine positive integral Hilbert degree. Its constant depends only on ambient coordinate count, and the bound is C(n)*degree^2*logarithmicBudget(H)^17*H^(1/degree). This holds uniformly for growing curve degrees, on the actual normalized integer point sets, and includes prime rational curves without assuming geometric integrality.

PresentedCurveDegreeCutoff constructs the actual low-degree subfamily and the actual exceptional point subset of an arbitrary finite normalized population covered by such curves. If their total real degree is at most b, then curves of degree at most M are retained and all other points form a subset with cardinality at most C(n)*b^2*logarithmicBudget(H)^17*H^(1/M). The proof uses the literal finite union of the high-degree curve point sets, the uniform individual curve bounds, and sum degree^2 <= (sum degree)^2. The retained family has no more members than the original family, every retained degree is at most M, and every original point outside the constructed exceptional subset lies on a retained curve. The cutoff M is arbitrary positive natural.

P3SingleAuxiliaryCurveCover applies these proved constructions to the original P3 hypersurface, original X3=1 integer points and one actual homogeneous auxiliary equation of degree e vanishing on that population. It gives at most e*d retained curves of degree at most M and the actual exceptional bound C(4)*(e*d)^2*logarithmicBudget(H)^17*H^(1/M). No point/curve correspondence, total degree sum or exception-set count is assumed.

This supplies a simpler route to the primary P3 cover: an auxiliary degree e=O_d(H^(1/sqrt(d))*log(H)+1), together with a fixed sufficiently large M and logarithmic absorption, has exactly the required two exponents. The global auxiliary equation of that quantitative degree has not yet been constructed. Thus no full primary cover is asserted at this checkpoint; canonical T1.7 remains conditional on the same exact primary P3 input. The uniform exponent assembly is the next step.
