# License scope

The Lean development and repository-authored build and packaging tools use
**Apache-2.0**, as provided in [LICENSE](LICENSE). Imported and ported material
retains the exact third-party licenses, source pins and attribution recorded
in its source headers and the `third_party/` directory.

Received manuscript packages, their historical archives, and the current P1
article and technical companion retain their existing rights and assistance
notices. The repository's software license is not a new manuscript-license
grant. No Creative Commons license is inferred from Paper C, which is a
different work. The author may select a manuscript license when publishing
the P1 paper and companion on Zenodo.

The `license: Apache-2.0` field in `CITATION.cff` and `.zenodo.json` describes
the software. A Zenodo paper record has its own manuscript-license choice.
The standalone source ZIP copies the existing manuscript bytes and includes
this distinction in its README. Its build helper is Apache-2.0; that does not
relicense the TeX text or PDFs.
