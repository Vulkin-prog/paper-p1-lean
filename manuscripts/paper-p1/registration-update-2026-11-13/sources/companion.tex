\begin{abstract}
This companion supplies the arithmetic and uniform counting estimates
for the article \emph{Sharp Poisson approximation and second-order
correction for long multiplicative runs}. The two texts prove the
explicit Poisson distance equivalent, the optimal quadratic error
power $4/3$, the third factorial cumulant power $41/30$, and the
hierarchy of errors for rational channels of fixed height.

The proofs are organized by the estimates they supply: exact pair
profiles, fixed-source and half-power counts, confinement and pair
rigidity, positive triple masses, and independent certificate tails.
A common homogeneous probability bounds the several weights used
in these arguments. The resulting consequences include positive Palm
costs for arbitrary target windows and a support count uniform in
its lower cardinality. Fixed-order and growing-cardinality estimates
retain distinct hypotheses throughout.
\end{abstract}
