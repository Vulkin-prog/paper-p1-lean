The accompanying

provides proofs for the 118 numbered results in the article and companion
(26 and 92, respectively). Its
\href{https://github.com/Vulkin-prog/paper-p1-lean/blob/e564207ab543c7c0967e34b52f3d456d7a8f302c/docs/manuscript-results-publication-2026-11-12.json}{statement index}
records the correspondence of clauses, definitions and inherited
hypotheses, including the conditional premise of
Theorem~\ref{p13:transfer:finite}.
The documentation of the recorded replay is fixed at commit
\href{https://github.com/Vulkin-prog/paper-p1-lean/tree/a1c8092f7e25acf7f3b78283039cfbd69491f88e}{\texttt{a1c8092f7e25}}.
The linked
\href{https://github.com/Vulkin-prog/paper-p1-lean/blob/a1c8092f7e25acf7f3b78283039cfbd69491f88e/docs/validation/v2.0/proof-source-manifest.json}{source manifest}
and
\href{https://github.com/Vulkin-prog/paper-p1-lean/blob/a1c8092f7e25acf7f3b78283039cfbd69491f88e/docs/validation/v2.0/validation.json}{validation record}
identify the proof source used for the recorded full replay as
\href{https://github.com/Vulkin-prog/paper-p1-lean/tree/aa12e797ce895f6b7aef6e4f1f0c6e5e9a58aab4}{\texttt{aa12e797ce89}}.
That record specifies Lean~4.36.0-rc2 and Mathlib commit
\href{https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/tree/9d581dfff2e41de989072ea63abf3daf69311520}{\texttt{9d581dfff2e4}},
and includes compilation results, extracted statement types and the
kernel-axiom audit. The documentation snapshot and the replayed proof
source have distinct roles.

The correspondence between the written and formal statements was
examined with AI assistance. It is not a machine translation of the
written proofs or a substitute for independent human review.
The conditional-sum and propagation clauses of
Proposition~\ref{p1:fixed:propagation} allow homogeneous prescriptions;
its small-contact clause uses the original run-start events. The formal
interfaces distinguish these scopes as well.

The last dossier uses three compound statements, retaining all thirteen
remaining main-result clauses and directly selecting companion
Propositions~T13.1 and~T15.1, with
the latter's displayed sequence corollary. The former controls the weighted
full-support sum at all cardinalities with one threshold before every subset
and minimum size. The latter shows that fixed factorial moments and uniform
exponential-moment control cannot replace growing-order estimates for a
polynomial total-variation rate; no multiplicative realization of that
counterexample is claimed. The other companion results supply proof
dependencies, without being claimed as all directly registered.
