\section*{Formalization and the version 1.8 revision}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Formalization and the version 1.8 revision}

The associated repository
\url{https://github.com/Vulkin-prog/paper-p1-lean}
contains the Lean development for the article and its companion,
checked with Lean~4.36.0-rc2 and the pinned Mathlib revision
\texttt{9d581dfff2e41de989072ea63abf3daf69311520}.
Version~1.8 is a working draft integrating selected strengthened
estimates and shorter proofs already present in that development.
It preserves the 167 numbered-result labels and their numbering.
The original version~1.6 sources remain preserved byte for byte.

\paragraph{The two statement corrections introduced in version 1.7.}
The original inventory has 165 valid statements with checked proofs
and two false original formulations. Their corrected replacements
are kept separate from those originals. These corrections are retained
here; the further strengthenings in version~1.8 are not additional
claims that the original statements were false.

In Lemma~\ref{p1:arith:asymmetric}, the alignment radius follows the
old enclosing interval $X+[-DB,DB]$: the exclusion is
$|rz-sX|\le D(r+s)B$. The centres satisfy
$N\le X<N+QN$, $z\in I_N$, with fixed $Q\ge1$, and the cutoff satisfies
$(2D+1)B\le H\le D_{\rm cut}B$. The weight $2^{\delta^*}-1$ and the
power $31/24$ are unchanged. A shifted $2:3$ family with
$L=3k$, $B=3k+1$, $X=2t$, $z=3t+3B$ and
$U_X=[X,X+3B]\cap\Z$ has $\delta^*\ge k-1$. It is nonaligned at
radius one for $t>5B^4$ but aligned at radius two; its dyadic mass
of order $N^{4/3}$ refutes the original radius-one formulation.
Compressed-source applications use the same enlarged radius on both
sides of the alignment partition.

In Lemma~\ref{p13:short:quadratic}, the counting exponent is capped:
\[
 c_2(\beta,\delta)
   =\min\{1,\beta-\delta,\max(\beta/2-\delta,1-\beta/2)\}.
\]
Its affine upper bound is then valid for every real $\beta,\delta$.
The uncapped expression still gives a valid counting estimate, but
its exact affine comparison requires $\beta-2\delta\le2$ when
$\delta\ge0$. This restriction is necessary on actual positive-divisor
data: squarefree consecutive $N,N+1$ with $h=e=1$ give
$\beta=\log(N(N+1))/\log N>2$ and $\delta=0$.
The extra entry $1$ is supplied by the trivial $O(N)$ count.

\paragraph{Scientific content and proof scope.}
The fixed-arity anchored confinement and primitive weights are proved
in Section~\ref{p14:comp:confinement} and
Proposition~\ref{p13:transfer:primitive}. The ordinary smooth-window
census is stated and proved near Proposition~\ref{p1:limits:all-anchors};
the finite cylinder refinements follow
Proposition~\ref{p1:limits:cylinder}; the exact reference-field
identities accompany Lemma~\ref{p1:limits:vector-reference}.
Their proofs retain the distinction between actual arithmetic
indicators and independent reference arrivals, and between fixed
orders and separately controlled growing ranges. The general sharp
short-average theorem, the Dickman relative-density asymptotic and
an unrestricted general surface estimate are not additional claims
of this revision.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
The repository records the original, corrected and strengthened
statements, their precise Lean declarations and validation sources.
The audited proofs use only the kernel axioms
\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
Finite diagnostic computations and kernel checking have distinct
roles; neither replaces checking that a formal statement represents
the printed mathematical claim. The mathematical change ledger
also retains intermediate proof constructions, which are not all
separate scientific contributions.
This working draft has no Palomar registration identifier.
