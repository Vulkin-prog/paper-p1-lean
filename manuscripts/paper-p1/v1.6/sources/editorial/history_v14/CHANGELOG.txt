P1 V1.4 -- 31 October 2026

Starting point: P1 V1.3, the 142-page cumulative manuscript delivered on
31 October 2026.

Editorial change
---------------
The work is now presented as a 60-page main article and a 93-page technical
companion, with a common version/date and cross-document references.
The article follows the exact-marginal proof directly. The companion
retains the complete regular-set 4/5 reconstruction and all extensions.

Conservation
------------
All 505 old labels, 167 numbered results and 166 proof environments are
retained across the two texts. Full correspondence tables are supplied.
Proofs moved to the companion are identified by the statement number in
the article; the article states quantitative hypotheses at the interface.

Notation and presentation
-------------------------
- Local rigidity moved beside the exact marginals.
- S_x is defined for all x in I_N; p_x=2^(-dim S_x) is restricted to
  admissible events.
- Affine identities use heterogeneous marginals explicitly; on the regular
  set their homogeneous specializations recover the old argument.
- Raw nullity and effective relative nullity are explicitly distinguished.
- Positional incident-edge intensity is iota_x, avoiding collision with d_x.
- The positional field definition now has an actual equation number.
- Four inherited malformed roman-type expressions were repaired.
- The channel table heading now says 'First omitted channels'.
- Chronological references, the uniformity guide and reading routes were
  rewritten for the two-document architecture.
- AI assistance and source information identify the V1.4 archive.

Verification
------------
Cross-reviews checked conservation and dependencies. Every PDF page was
visually inspected; key dense pages were also inspected at full resolution.
All internal and cross-document link targets were checked. The heterogeneous
and stopped finite diagnostics were rerun; other V1.3 finite outputs remain
available with their original scope. Clean source-archive compilation and
final artifact hashes are recorded in production_checks.json.

No new asymptotic exponent, formal proof certification, independent human
verification, journal submission or publication is claimed by this revision.
