The smallest displayed margin is $1/120$. In contrast, two pair
activities have size $A_2^2\ll N^{-4/3+\eta}$ and are retained at
the target scale. They have no strict power saving to spend.
For a prescribed final accuracy $\varepsilon_0>0$, one may choose
$0<\tau<\min(\varepsilon_0/4,1/960)$ and invoke completed auxiliary
estimates with total loss at most $\tau$ each. Their own input margins
are chosen smaller as needed; in a product, divide the allotted loss
between the factors. Thus the same symbol $\varepsilon$ in successive
statements never requires the same numerical value at every stage.
Rank thresholds and fixed geometric degrees have separate roles.
The fixed-order bounds are used only through order $256$; larger
cardinalities have the uniform certificate estimate and a factorial
boundary bound.

For clarity, the rank threshold $\eta=1/30$ and the weak-pair threshold
$\theta<\eta/200$ in this partition are distinct from an error margin.
With final accuracy $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{out}}>0$, choose a fixed
$0<\tau<\min(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{out}}/4,1/960)$, and allocate
smaller input losses so that every completed sector loses at most
$\tau$. The nonaligned target above a rational pair then retains the
margin $1/120-\tau>0$, and the incompatible-triple error retains
$2/15-\tau>0$. The other nondominant sectors have larger margins.
All offset and residual-rank losses are included in these completed
sector budgets before $N$ is taken large. In particular the latter
error is negligible in the positive rational lower bound as well as
small enough for the upper bound.

The margin needed for this sign argument is
$3/2-4/3=1/6$. With a fixed total error loss $\tau<1/6$,
\[
 \frac{N^{-3/2+\tau}}{\alpha_N^2}
 \ll_C L^{-2}N^{-1/6+\tau}\longrightarrow0.
\]
For the norm lower bound, the subsequent choice of an integer $k$ with
$4/[3(k-1)]\leq\varepsilon_{\mathrm{out}}$ is made after the final
accuracy is specified and before $N$. No uniformity in a growing
moment order is required.

Write $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{out}}$ for the requested final margin and
choose $0<\tau<\min(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{out}}/4,1/960)$.
Invoke reconstruction with accuracy $\tau$ and the arithmetic activity
estimate with accuracy $\tau$. Their product is
\[
 N^\tau A_3\ll N^{-41/30+2\tau}
     =N^{-4/3-(1/30-2\tau)}.
\]
Since $1/30-2\tau>0$ and the other term is
$N^{-4/3+\tau}$, the required upper bound follows.
The lower bound uses its own fixed Charlier interpolation order,
as specified in Theorem~\ref{p13:lower:optimal}.
