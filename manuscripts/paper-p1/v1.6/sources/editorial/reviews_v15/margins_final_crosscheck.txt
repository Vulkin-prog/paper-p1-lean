1. MAIN ERROR MARGINS
The displayed exact margins 1/120, 2/15, 1/30, 1/60, 1/6 agree with the estimates they compare. The text correctly distinguishes a final requested accuracy from the total loss allotted to a completed contribution. It explicitly budgets products, so A2^2 need not inherit an unaccounted second loss. Choosing 0<tau<min(epsilon_out/4,1/960) makes both relevant assembled gaps strictly positive: 1/120-tau and 1/30-2*tau. The geometric thresholds eta=1/30 and theta<eta/200 remain distinct from these accuracy parameters.

2. QUARTIC ARGUMENT AND DEPENDENCIES
The ratio L^{-2} N^{-1/6+tau} tends to zero for fixed tau<1/6, exactly as required to preserve the positive alpha^2 term. The Charlier order is fixed after final accuracy, rather than allowed to grow with N. The revised map now includes fixed-source counts in the quartic inputs and does not make reconstruction an input to its own lower bound.

3. DETERMINANT LEDGER T1.4
The order is acyclic: final error -> zeta -> auxiliary bidegrees -> polynomial-height exponents (including the enlarged kappa after lattice changes) -> curve-count epsilon'. The condition (C+kappa)epsilon'<=zeta pays for point-height and coefficient-height losses. The input coefficient sizes can vary with N but their height exponents and all degrees are fixed first. C and the final enlarged kappa must be read as the bounds for the completed lattice transformation; the adjacent Steps 2–3 provide this interpretation. No uniformity in a growing degree is asserted.

4. GEOMETRIC LEDGER T1.7
The first branch fixes A0,K and accuracy, then D, then k, then the external error margins. The degree-one branch fixes q, then E, then k satisfying both displayed inequalities, then the internal error margin. Increasing k changes only fixed dimensions/degrees/constants and does not increase the per-equation input exponent A0. This agrees with the coefficient paragraph in T11.1. The arbitrary-coefficient per-system estimate is kept separate from the N^{o(1)} pattern enumeration. The table does not charge a coefficient-dependent external constant where uniformity is required.

5. TWO SMALL WORDING RECOMMENDATIONS SENT TO ROOT
(a) In §1.5, replace “The fixed-order bounds are used only through order 256” by wording specifically referring to the fixed connected-activity estimates in reconstruction. Independent moments used for Charlier interpolation may have fixed orders larger than 256.
(b) At the end of the main theorem proof in §17, replace “Every cutoff ... was fixed ... before N” by wording referring to auxiliary accuracy parameters and fixed orders. K_N is explicitly a growing cardinality cutoff; its defining rule, rather than its value, is fixed.
These are clarifications of scope, not gaps in the estimates.

Limit: this check verifies consistency of the new parameter and margin exposition with the displayed proofs. It is not a new independent proof of the external arithmetic inputs.
