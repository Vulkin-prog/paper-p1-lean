31 October 2026
Scope: structural reorganization of eight V1.3 source files; no new asymptotic claim.

Files owned

affine_algebra, stopped, fixed_moments, cores, closure, exact_setup,
exact_transfer, quartic_lower.
The local-rigidity lemma and proof from limits_appendix were moved into

Conservation
All 190 original labels from the eight files were found exactly once across
our main/companion versions. The two local-rigidity labels were also found
once. Root's complete 505-label audit checks the full assembly.
Of 50 original proof environments, 45 remain verbatim. The five edited proofs
are the first three affine-identity proofs (heterogeneous formulation),
conditional propagation (factor 2 from p_x <= 2p), and the first-approximation
proof (its independent reference-continuity calculation was extracted into
main closure and replaced by an explicit cross-reference). Every other
proof was retained intact, possibly relocated. A machine-readable list is in
probability_proof_preservation.json. This checks preservation, not validity.

Main article contents
- exact_setup: exact effective spaces, marginals p_x in {p,2p} for admissible
  windows, relative nullity, exact cumulant identities, and complete local
  rigidity proof near its use. Fixed-source extension via linear sections
  remains explicit, with fixed source/target number.
- affine_algebra: complete centered Bernoulli, full-support Fourier,
  compatible-nullity and canonical-component identities. They now use exact
  heterogeneous marginals from the start; the original p-specialization
  follows on the regular set. The algebraic proofs were correspondingly
  updated, rather than merely changing notation in their statements.
- stopped: the general finite hereditary identity, positive remainder kernel,
  and fully dependent suffix example. The application-specific regular
  two-channel class is in the companion.
- fixed_moments: disjoint-recovery lemma and proof, fixed-source target
  statement, conditional propagation and proof, independent fixed-moment
  theorem statement. Detailed fixed-source curve/CRT arguments, primitive
  classification, compressed primitive estimate, and moment proof are in
  companion fixed_moments.
- cores: exact kernel/count/degree/generator interfaces and their geometric
  definitions. Complete elementary proofs plus the old all-cardinality
  4/5 results are in companion cores.
- closure: independent Lipschitz, positivity, and Charlier-norm facts. The
  old first approximation and the downstream TV deduction are in companion
  closure; no dependence on the final law is hidden in upstream reference
  lemmas.
- exact_transfer: the exact Bernoulli reference INCLUDING every covariance,
  conversion, primitive mass, independent extension/pair deficits,
  fixed-connected reduction, explicit uniform tails after 256, stopped
  assembly, and final transfer proof remain. Detailed dense-target and
  small-kernel proofs are relocated to companion exact_transfer, with
  precise statements and a dense-count proof outline retained in main.
- quartic_lower: retained in full, including positive compatible contribution,
  cancellation of disconnected terms, the unbounded Charlier test and its
  independent fixed-moment interpolation, and the limitation for Hermite.

Dependency order
The main article may read exact_setup, affine_algebra, stopped, fixed_moments,
cores, closure, then the arithmetic half-count/rigidity/triple modules,
exact_transfer, quartic_lower. Root inserts the pair arithmetic section and
consequences at the appropriate places.
Companion fixed_moments depends only on the arithmetic inputs and the main
fixed-source statements; it proves bounded fixed moments without any
reconstruction theorem. Companion cores uses those moments, main algebra,
and certificate tools. Companion stopped defines the regular retained class;
companion closure uses it and the old core bounds. Its TV-corollary proof is
explicitly downstream of the final main theorem. Companion exact_transfer
uses the earlier arithmetic geometric lemmas, not the transfer conclusion.

Critical scope checks
- No fixed-order constant is substituted at an order growing with N.
- The 256 cutoff is a sufficient fixed cutoff, not an optimal constant.
- The all-cardinality pair deficit remains N^{-3/2+epsilon}.
- The conversion N^{-5/3+epsilon} applies to B_2 with all covariances,
  not to the bare independent Bernoulli sum.
- Effective relative rank excludes counting local defects twice.
- The independent fixed-moment proof remains upstream of the quartic test.
- The quartic lower bound is specific to the quadratic reference; it does
  not establish the same optimal lower exponent for Hermite.
- The regular 4/5 construction remains complete for positional/deletion uses;
  it is not used as a prerequisite for the exact scalar reconstruction.

Typographical corrections

had become a literal newline followed by 'm odd' or 'm uniform in'. They were
changed to \mathrm{odd} and \mathrm{uniform\ in}, respectively. No mathematical
quantity was changed.

Review limit
This is a conservation and dependency audit of the split. It does not amount
to a new independent proof of every inherited arithmetic estimate, a formal
verification, or proof-assistant certification.
