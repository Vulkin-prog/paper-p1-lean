Date: 31 October 2026
Scope: editorial reorganization of six V1.3 sections; no new mathematical claim.

The exact setup, effective event spaces, relative nullity, and fixed-source

COMPANION INCLUDE ORDER
The same order is valid once the companion's foundational arithmetic and

interfaces are explicit and resolve via the common reference mechanism.

ALLOCATION
Short supports: main has the growing-degree moment statement and the full
conditional character/Hölder argument; companion has the quadratic and split
kernel lemmas and complete moment enumeration.
Half-counts: main has the precise uniform theorem, an explanation of the
inductive mechanism, and full connected/primitive and pair consequences;
companion has all small-kernel, smooth-modulus, certificate, and iteration
proofs. The definition of K_H is explicitly retained in main because the
confinement section uses it.
Triple activities: main keeps the independent-target deficit statement and
the full signed identities for incompatible triples and positive activity;
companion has the finite pair partition and proof of the deficit.
Confinement: main states the near-primitive and strong nonaligned triple
interfaces, together with the determinant/group mechanism. Companion has
the confinement lemma, dense primitive and pair results, and full proofs
of the two interfaces.
Pair rigidity: main retains the exact square-class definitions, short-basis
theorem, common-curve theorem, full integer-grid argument, degree-uniform
BCN statement, full growing-degree contradiction, aligned bound with the
exact-contact explanation, and full triple-nullity/probability consequence.
Companion has the local estimates, short-basis extraction, factor heights,
biquadratic auxiliary construction, common-curve resultants, and full
aligned-rank proof.
Third cumulant: main retains the 19/30 geometry and proof, the aligned-family
statement and strong-pair consequence, the complete compatible 10:12:15
construction, and the full four-case proof of the 41/30 theorem. The long
normalization and residual-kernel proof for all triples above finitely many
aligned channels is in the companion.

MATHEMATICAL SCOPE RETAINED
- r, s and logarithmic block widths fixed before N for the half-count.
- Source blocks may overlap or be dependent, and new projections need not
  remain nonzero modulo the old space.
- The short-selection bound is uniform to c log log N, not only at fixed
  external degree.
- The common-curve constant is independent of the number of basis vectors.
- q_0^2 log B = o(log N), a < 1/2, and the degree-uniform BCN estimate are
  explicit in main; the sufficient rank constant is not claimed sharp.
- Exact compatible separation is L, including the contact distance L.
- The rational coincidence inequality is separate from the residual-kernel
  estimate. The true-window 10:12:15 population includes an explicit affine
  compatibility check and a positive centered coefficient.
- 41/30 is an optimal power for the factorial cumulant, with no claim of an
  asymptotic constant or logarithmic factor for the full cumulant.
- The 1/30 gap below the 4/3 remainder scale does not assert a higher-order
  approximation theorem.

TYPOGRAPHICAL REPAIR
The inherited pair_rigidity.tex contained two literal carriage returns in
place of the intended LaTeX command \rm: in 'odd' and 'nonaligned' predicates.
The V1.4 files restore \rm. This changes no mathematical assertion.
