The following probability-weight paragraph, beginning 'Projection injectivity also gives' and ending 'none gives a growing-r estimate', repeats the new proposition's proof. It can be replaced by a short forward pointer:

'The conversion of this host count to homogeneous probabilities and the three original weights is given in Proposition~\ref{p13:transfer:primitive}. That proposition uses the host count proved here, together with the conditional half-count, and treats the weight comparison only at fixed arity.'

This change is optional editorial simplification, not a correction needed for validity.
