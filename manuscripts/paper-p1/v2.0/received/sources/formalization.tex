\section*{Formal proofs and this edition}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Formal proofs and this edition}
The associated development is available at
\url{https://github.com/Vulkin-prog/paper-p1-lean}.
It contains Lean proofs checked with Lean~4.36.0-rc2 and a pinned
Mathlib revision, together with statement correspondence and axiom audits.
The present exposition reorganizes the arguments and includes two
consequences obtained by composing existing estimates: the positive
Palm bound for arbitrary target windows and the uniform full-support
bound of Proposition~\ref{p1:uniform:full-support}.
The new numbering and these composed statements have not been replayed
as a revised Lean manuscript interface. The source package records the
correspondence and the remaining alignment work.
