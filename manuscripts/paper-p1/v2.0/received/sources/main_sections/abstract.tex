\begin{abstract}
Let \(f\) be completely multiplicative with independent fair signs
at primes. We count starts of constant runs of length at least
\(L\) in \([N,2N)\), with \(L=\log_2N+O(1)\) and both signs
included. The total variation distance to the Poisson law with
nominal or exact mean has an explicit equivalent of order
\(LN^{-2/3}\), with a coefficient depending on \(N2^{-L}\) and
\(L\bmod3\). After retaining the exact mean and second factorial
cumulant, the quadratic Poisson--Charlier approximation has optimal
error power \(4/3\): for every fixed \(\varepsilon>0\), its
\(\ell^1\) error lies between constant multiples of
\(N^{-4/3-\varepsilon}\) and \(N^{-4/3+\varepsilon}\), uniformly
in each fixed critical band.

The leading correction comes from rational pairs. Keeping all
channels up to a fixed height \(h\) gives an explicit distance
equivalent with power \(h/(h+1)\), determined by the first omitted
height. The third factorial cumulant has optimal power \(41/30\).
The proof expands around the exact Bernoulli marginals and stops at
the first excluded dependent configuration. Arithmetic bounds for
fixed configurations and a separate uniform tail estimate control
the resulting positive boundary mass. Short square relations and
a common algebraic curve bound the triple interactions; a fourth
Charlier test detects the two-pair contribution that makes the
quadratic exponent optimal.
\end{abstract}
