\section*{Formal proofs and this edition}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Formal proofs and this edition}
The associated development is available at
\url{https://github.com/Vulkin-prog/paper-p1-lean}.
The aligned edition of 12 November 2026 maps all 118 numbered results
(26 in the article and 92 in the companion) to Lean proofs checked
with Lean~4.36.0-rc2 and a pinned Mathlib revision.
The source and compiled-type index records every clause and inherited
hypothesis, including the printed conditional finite-transfer principle.
The repository preserves the received edition, earlier manuscripts,
proof-replay records and kernel axiom audits. Results outside the present
scalar-count scope remain in the historical library.

\Needspace{14\baselineskip}

the earlier planned scopes. The following numbers refer to the main article:
