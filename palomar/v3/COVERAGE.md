The literal prime-sign model is defined in every dossier that needs it. Both
word signs are counted, with the predecessor condition that prevents counting
interior shifts. Original heterogeneous marginals and incompatible events are
retained. No abstract premise restating the desired asymptotic estimate is used
to discharge an unconditional theorem.

The companion is proof evidence used by the substantive library. Some of its
results are also selected foundations, particularly exact cumulant identities.
This plan does **not** claim direct registration of all 92 companion results,
un-numbered exposition, numerical experiments or future-work discussion.

Historical out-of-scope work, including positional fields and exponential
cylinders, stays proved in the repository. Those old planned registrations are
not added to the six V2 dossiers. Thus the six current records deliberately
track the adopted scalar-count article rather than all historical extensions.
